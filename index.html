Our Story

Crafted in Rwanda, Loved Worldwide

Made in Rwanda Crochet was born from a passion for preserving traditional craftsmanship while creating contemporary fashion and lifestyle products. Every piece is handmade by skilled Rwandan artisans who pour creativity, cultural heritage, and meticulous attention into every stitch.

We believe in slow fashion — quality over quantity. Our products are made with premium materials, designed to last, and infused with the vibrant spirit of Rwanda.
