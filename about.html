About Us

Our Story

From the hills of Rwanda to homes around the world — a journey of passion, culture, and craftsmanship.

Born in Rwanda, Crafted with Purpose

Made in Rwanda Crochet began with a simple vision: to celebrate the rich textile traditions of Rwanda while creating beautiful, contemporary products that people love to wear and display in their homes.

Founded in Kigali, our brand brings together a community of talented women artisans who have mastered the art of crochet over years of dedicated practice. Each artisan works from home or in our cooperative workshop, earning fair wages while preserving a craft that connects them to their heritage.

What sets us apart is our commitment to authenticity. We don't mass-produce. We don't cut corners. Every bag, garment, hat, and home piece is made by hand, one stitch at a time, with the same care you'd expect from something made for family.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Everything we create is guided by principles that honor our artisans, our culture, and our customers.

Authenticity

Every product is genuinely handmade in Rwanda. No factories, no shortcuts — just real craftsmanship you can see and feel.

Premium Quality

We source the finest cotton and acrylic yarns, ensuring our products are durable, soft, and beautifully finished.

Cultural Pride

Our designs draw from Imigongo patterns, Agaseke basket motifs, and the vibrant colors of Rwanda's landscapes.

Community Impact

We empower women artisans with fair wages, flexible work, and skills training — building stronger communities in Rwanda.

Sustainability

Slow fashion by nature. We create timeless pieces meant to last, reducing waste and celebrating mindful consumption.

Customer Love

From custom orders to personalized packaging, we treat every customer like family — because you are part of our story.
